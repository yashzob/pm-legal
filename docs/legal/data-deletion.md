# Data Deletion Instructions

**Python & Maths in Prayagraj** · Last updated: 25 October 2026

You can ask us to delete your data at any time. Choose the case that applies to you.

## If you are a business (Customer) using our platform

**Option A: do it yourself**
1. Sign in to the platform and open **WhatsApp → Bots**.
2. Turn the bot off, then delete it. This removes its stored access tokens and secrets from our vault.
3. Delete contacts, uploads and campaigns you no longer need from their pages.
4. To disconnect WhatsApp from Meta's side too, go to **Meta Business Settings → Integrations → Connected apps** and remove our app.

**Option B: ask us**
Email **y.yashvardhan01@gmail.com** from your account email with the subject **"Delete my data"**, and tell us whether you want specific items removed or your entire account closed. We will confirm the request and complete it within **30 days**.

## If you removed our app from Facebook or Meta

Removing the app in your Facebook or Meta settings stops our access immediately. To also have us delete the information we already hold, email **y.yashvardhan01@gmail.com** with the subject **"Delete my data"** and the Facebook name and email on your account.

## If you are a person who messaged a business on WhatsApp (End User)

That business decides what to do with your data, so please ask them first. You can also email us at **y.yashvardhan01@gmail.com** with the phone number you used (with country code) and subject **"Delete my data"**. We will remove it from our systems, or forward your request to the business that is responsible for it, and tell you which one we did.

To stop receiving messages, reply **STOP** to the business, or block the number in WhatsApp.

## What gets deleted

- Your account and profile details.
- WhatsApp connection details and access tokens.
- Contacts, messages, media and campaign records held for you.

## What may be kept

- Backups, which are overwritten within 90 days.
- Records we are legally required to keep (for example billing and security audit logs).

Questions? Email **y.yashvardhan01@gmail.com**.
