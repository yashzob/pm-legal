# Privacy Policy

**Python & Maths in Prayagraj** ("we", "us", "our") · Last updated: 25 October 2026
Contact: y.yashvardhan01@gmail.com

This policy explains what personal information our WhatsApp messaging platform (the "Service") collects, why, and what choices you have. The Service lets businesses ("Customers") connect their WhatsApp Business number, send campaigns and template messages, and run automated or human-assisted replies with their own customers ("End Users").

## 1. Who is responsible for which data

- **For our Customers' account data** (their staff logins, WhatsApp connection details, billing contact), we are the data controller.
- **For End User data** (the people who message a Customer's WhatsApp number), the Customer decides why and how it is used. We process it on the Customer's behalf as a data processor. If you are an End User and want to access or delete your data, please contact the business that messaged you first; you may also contact us (section 9).

## 2. Information we collect

**From Customers and their staff**
- Name, work email address, password (stored hashed), role and permissions.
- Company name and settings.
- Facebook / Meta account details shared when connecting WhatsApp: your name and profile picture, plus the WhatsApp Business Account ID, phone number ID, display name and phone number you choose to connect.
- Access tokens issued by Meta for your WhatsApp account. These are stored in an encrypted secrets vault, not in our regular database, and are never shown back to you in full.

**From End Users (processed for the Customer)**
- Phone number and WhatsApp profile name.
- Message content, media you send or receive, delivery and read status, and timestamps.
- Contact details the Customer uploads (for example names, tags, and opt-in or opt-out status).

**Automatically**
- Technical and security logs (IP address, browser type, time of access) and an audit log of important account actions.

We do not collect your Facebook friends list, posts, or any Facebook data beyond what is listed above.

## 3. How we use information

- To connect your WhatsApp Business number and send and receive messages through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform.
- To run campaigns, template messages and bot replies that you configure.
- To provide AI-generated replies when a Customer enables that feature (see section 5).
- To authenticate users, keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, and keep audit records.
- To support Customers, fix problems, and improve the Service.
- To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use End User messages for advertising.

## 4. Meta and WhatsApp

The Service uses Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. When you connect a number, Meta processes your data under its own terms and privacy policy ([Meta Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/), [WhatsApp Business Terms](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/business-terms)). Messages travel through Meta's servers. You can revoke our access at any time in your Meta Business settings (Business Settings → Integrations → Connected apps).

## 5. AI features

If a Customer turns on AI replies, the text of the relevant conversation and the Customer's own instructions and knowledge base documents are sent to an AI model provider selected by the Customer, in order to generate a reply. The provider is bound to process this only to return the response. Customers should tell their End Users when an automated assistant is replying.

## 6. Who else receives information

We share information only with:
- **Meta Platforms** (WhatsApp delivery), as described above.
- **AI model providers** chosen by the Customer, as described in section 5.
- **Infrastructure and hosting providers** who store and process data for us under confidentiality obligations.
- **Authorities**, when required by law or to protect rights, safety and security.
- A **successor** if our business is sold or reorganised, with notice to you.

## 7. Retention

- Customer account data is kept while the account is active.
- Message and contact data is kept until the Customer deletes it or closes the account, then removed from our systems within 30 days, except for limited backups (up to 90 days) and records we must keep by law.
- Access tokens are deleted from the vault when a WhatsApp connection is removed or the bot is deleted.

## 8. Security

Secrets are kept in a dedicated vault; each Customer's data is stored in its own database schema; access is permission-controlled and important actions are audit-logged. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Your rights and how to contact us

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and similar laws), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a regulator. To exercise these rights, email **y.yashvardhan01@gmail.com** with the phone number or email concerned. See also our [Data Deletion Instructions](data-deletion). We respond within 30 days.

End Users can also stop receiving messages at any time by replying **STOP** to a business message, or by blocking the number in WhatsApp.

## 10. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

## 11. International transfers

Data may be processed in countries other than yours, including where Meta and our providers operate. We take steps to protect it as described in this policy.

## 12. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify account owners by email or in the app.
